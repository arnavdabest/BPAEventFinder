Common questions

How does the quiz pick my events?

Every event is tagged by format (test, hands-on, project, or live presentation), solo or team, subject, prep time, and difficulty. Each answer adds or subtracts points, and the results follow BPA's entry rules: 2 main events, at least one of them solo, plus optional add-ons.

An event's info looks wrong or outdated

Event info comes from BPA's "Events at a Glance" (September 2026), and the "More about this event" sections are summaries. Check the official guidelines at bpa.org, then report the mistake with the button above so we can fix it.

Is this the official BPA website?

No. It's a free tool built by a chapter member. For official rules, registration, and deadlines go to bpa.org or talk to your chapter advisor.

What data do you collect? Can I turn it off?

Only anonymous quiz activity: no names, emails, or IP addresses. You can turn tracking off or delete your data on the Privacy page.

Can my chapter link to this or use it at meetings?

Yes, please do. Add ?utm_source=yourchapter to the end of the link so we can see which chapters use it.
