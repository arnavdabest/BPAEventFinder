Event Matcher

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Short version: we save your quiz answers anonymously so we can see which BPA events students are interested in and make the quiz better. We never ask for your name, email, or school, we don't store IP addresses, we don't run ads, and we never sell or share the data.

Who we are

BPA Event Matcher ("we", "us") is a free, student-built tool made by a BPA chapter member. It is not run by or affiliated with Business Professionals of America, Inc. Questions about this policy go through our Support page.

What we collect

When you use the site, your browser sends anonymous usage data to our server:

DataExamples
Quiz activityThe answers you pick, how long each question takes, if you go back and change an answer, where you stop
Results and browsingThe events recommended to you, which event cards you open and for how long, links you click
Device infoPhone, tablet, or desktop; operating system and browser name; screen size; dark mode; language; time zone
Rough locationCountry and state or province, looked up by our host from your connection. We do not store your IP address or city.
How you found usThe referring website and any campaign tag in the link (like ?utm_source=instagram)
Anonymous IDA random code saved in your browser's local storage so repeat visits can be counted. It is not linked to who you are.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, school names, account logins, precise location, IP addresses, or anything you type outside the quiz buttons. The site has no accounts and no sign-up.

Why we collect it

  • To see which events, interests, and answers are most popular
  • To find confusing questions or places where people give up, and fix them
  • To keep the site working and protect it from abuse

We don't use the data for advertising, profiling individuals, or automated decisions about you.

Who can see it

Only the site's maintainer. Data is stored with these service providers, who process it on our behalf:

  • Vercel hosts the website and receives standard web request data. Vercel's privacy policy
  • Supabase stores the anonymous quiz data in the United States. Supabase's privacy policy
  • Google Fonts serves the site's font, so your browser contacts Google when the page loads. Google's privacy policy

We do not sell, rent, or trade data. We may share summary numbers (for example "40% of students picked a team event") with BPA chapters or advisors. We would only disclose raw data if the law required it.

How long we keep it

Data is deleted automatically after 2 years, or sooner if you delete it below.

Children

This site is made for high school BPA members and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and the data we collect can't identify you. If you're a parent or guardian with a concern, contact us through the Support page and we'll help.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. The database only accepts new anonymous records from the site and can't be read from the public internet. Access is limited to the maintainer's accounts.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, the EU, or the UK), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the use of data about you. Because we don't know who you are, the delete button above is the fastest way to use these rights. You can also contact us through the Support page.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will be noted on the home page.
